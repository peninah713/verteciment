Who we are

A specialist partner.
A practical perspective.

Verteciments Consulting is a Kenya-based consultancy focused on cement production, quality and sustainable product development.

We work with cement manufacturers to understand how raw materials, operating conditions and testing practices influence product performance.

That understanding shapes advice suited to each operation. Our recommendations bring technical analysis together with hands-on industry experience, balancing product quality, operational cost and environmental responsibility.
