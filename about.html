About Verteciments Consulting

Rooted in science.
Connected to industry.

We connect material science with practical plant experience to help cement manufacturers make informed decisions.

Who we are

A specialist partner
for the cement industry.

Verteciments Consulting is a Kenya-based consultancy focused on cement production, quality and sustainable product development.

We work with cement manufacturers to understand the relationship between raw materials, production conditions and product performance. That understanding shapes recommendations suited to each operation—not a generic set of answers.

Our work brings together technical analysis and hands-on industry experience, with attention to the balance between quality, operational cost and environmental responsibility.

The expertise behind the advice

Technical understanding.
Practical perspective.

Dr. Munyao and the Verteciments team focus on practical, science-backed solutions for cement and construction industry challenges.

Our perspective is shaped by working inside cement plants and understanding the conditions under which technical recommendations must perform.

Areas of technical focus

  • Cement chemistry and product performance
  • Alternative supplementary cementitious materials
  • Testing reliability and quality assurance
  • Low-carbon cement development
