Welcome To Verteciments Consulting

Verteciments Consulting is a leading cement consultancy firm based in Kenya, specializing in optimizing cement production, quality enhancement, and sustainable product development. Our team of experts provides tailored solutions to help cement manufacturers improve efficiency, reduce costs, and transition towards low-carbon cement manufacturing.

We specialize in the following:

  • Training & Quality Assurance
  • Process Optimization
  • Product Development & Innovation
  • Cement Additive, Grinding Aid Identification & Selection
  • Regulatory Compliance & Approvals
  • Laboratory Standardization
  • Concrete & Admixture Solutions
  • Identification & characterization of alternative pozzolanic materials to lower clinker consumption and enable efficient operations.

Why Choose Verteciments Consulting

Dr. Munyao and his team specialize in practical, science-backed solutions for the cement and construction industry, offering expertise in:

Developing low-carbon cements to meet environmental regulations and reduce carbon footprints.
Transforming agricultural and industrial waste into high-performance supplementary cementitious materials (SCMs).
Using microbial remediation (bacteria-based repair) to extend the lifespan of concrete structures.
Supporting companies in new product development, testing, and commercialization.
Enhancing manufacturing efficiency, product quality, and compliance through advanced material science.
