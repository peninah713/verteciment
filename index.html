Welcome to Verteciments Consulting

Verteciments Consulting is a leading cement consultancy firm based in Kenya, specializing in optimizing cement production, quality enhancement, and sustainable product development. Our team provides tailored solutions to help manufacturers improve efficiency, reduce costs, and transition towards low-carbon cement manufacturing.

Our areas of expertise include:

  • Training and quality assurance
  • Process optimization
  • Product development and innovation
  • Cement additive and grinding aid identification and selection
  • Regulatory compliance and approvals
  • Laboratory standardization
  • Concrete and admixture solutions
  • Identifying and characterizing alternative pozzolanic materials to lower clinker consumption and improve operations

Why Choose Verteciments Consulting?

Dr. Munyao and his team provide practical, science-backed solutions for the cement and construction industry.

Developing low-carbon cements that help reduce emissions and meet environmental requirements.
Transforming agricultural and industrial waste into high-performance supplementary cementitious materials (SCMs).
Applying microbial remediation and other repair approaches to help extend the service life of concrete structures.
Supporting companies with product development, testing, and commercialization.
Improving manufacturing efficiency, product quality, and compliance through applied materials science.
