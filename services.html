Our Services

At Verteciments Consulting, we help cement manufacturers improve efficiency, quality and sustainability with practical solutions built on real plant experience.

What we do

Six areas of expertise covering the cement production chain, from the laboratory to approvals and product development.

Training & Quality Assurance

We train quality control staff in advanced cement testing methods, including simplified, technically proven alternative methods for determining compressive strength at all curing ages.

We also cover pozzolanic activity determination for novel alternative supplementary cementitious materials (SCMs).

Enquire about training

Process Optimization

We help manufacturers significantly reduce the clinker factor while maintaining high product performance. This delivers cost savings and lower carbon emissions, in line with SDG 13.

Enquire about optimization

Product Development & Innovation

We guide our clients in developing new cement products that meet evolving customer needs, with a focus on:

  • Low-carbon cement formulations.
  • Performance enhancement through alternative materials.
Enquire about product development

Regulatory Compliance & Approvals

We guide new entrants in obtaining the necessary approvals from local authorities and ensuring compliance with industry standards.

Enquire about approvals

Cement Additive & Grinding Aid Selection

  • Grinding aids: With in-depth knowledge of cement additives, we help manufacturers select the most effective grinding aids and performance enhancers for their production processes.
  • Concrete admixtures and precast: We guide ready-mix and precast customers in designing suitable concrete mixes using the right admixtures.
Enquire about additives

Laboratory Standardization

We develop and implement standard operating procedures (SOPs) for cement and concrete laboratories, ensuring consistency and reliability in testing.

Enquire about lab standardization
