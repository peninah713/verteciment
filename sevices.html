Our Services

At Verteciments Consulting, we empower businesses with tailored solutions designed to drive growth, efficiency, and innovation.

Training & Quality Assurance

We offer specialized training for quality control staff on advanced cement testing methodologies, including and not limited to simplified & technical Pozzolanic activity determination on novel alternative supplementary Cementous materials (SCM).

Process Optimization

Our expertise in cement production allows us to help manufacturers reduce the clinker factor Reduce clinker factor significantly, while maintaining a high product performance. This results in cost savings and reduced carbon emissions in line with SDG 13.

Product Development & Innovation

We guide our clients in the development of new cement products that cater to evolving customer needs, with a focus on:

  • Low-carbon cement formulations.
  • Performance enhancement through alternative materials.

Regulatory Compliance & Approvals

We guide new entrants in obtaining necessary approvals from local authorities, ensuring compliance with industry standards.

Cement Additive & Grinding Aid Selection

  • Grinding Aid: With in-depth knowledge of cement additives, we help manufacturers select the most effective grinding aids and performance enhancers for their production processes.
  • Concrete admixtures and precast custom: We guide ready mix and precast customers in designing suitable concrete mixes by using the right admixtures.

Laboratory Standardization

We develop and implement standard operating procedures (SOPs) for cement and concrete laboratories to ensure consistency and reliability in testing.
