Our Story

Pioneering sustainable cement solutions for a greener future.

How a chemist’s frustration with the gap between research and the plant floor became a consultancy built on practical, science-backed change.

Who we are

Cement is pivotal to a sustainable world.

At Verteciments Consulting, we believe the cement industry is pivotal to building a sustainable world.

Founded by Dr. Onesmus Mulwa Munyao, a chemist and industry leader with more than 15 years of cement R&D expertise, we bridge cutting-edge science and real-world applications to drive innovation in construction materials.

Our mission

To transform cement from a carbon challenge into a climate solution through innovation, efficiency and sustainable practices.

Our vision

To pioneer a future where cement is a cornerstone of sustainable construction: innovation for net-zero impact without compromise.

Our founder’s vision

Closing the gap between the lab and the plant.

Dr. Munyao’s career began in the labs of Kenya’s leading cement manufacturers, where he spearheaded breakthroughs in low-carbon formulations and waste-to-value material science.

Frustrated by the gap between academic research and industrial execution, he launched Verteciments Consulting to empower companies with actionable, sustainable strategies that reduce emissions, enhance durability and align with global climate goals.

What drives us

Sustainability meets innovation.
